## What “complete” includes, and what it does not

Included: every objective printed on the October 2026 ACE and PCA exam guides, the commands for those tasks, the failure behavior those questions use, the six Well-Architected pillars, and every product family on the Cloud documentation home page at least at the weight the exams require.

Not included, because it is not a study guide: a paste of Google’s full documentation site, the copyrighted case-study PDFs, or a manual for industry products the exam guide does not score. When a flag in a lab is rejected, use `gcloud help` for that command. Flags move. The behavior in these chapters is what the question is about.
